# Sample photo credits

`scripts/screenshots.mjs` uses these two photos for the smoke test, and
`golden-silk-orbweaver.jpg` also for the store images. Both are real
observations, resized.

- `golden-silk-orbweaver.jpg` (in the store images): a female golden silk
  orb-weaver (*Trichonephila clavipes*) with a male above her, Anahuac National
  Wildlife Refuge, Texas, July 16, 2022, by Dario Taraborelli (radrat) via
  [iNaturalist](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/126590005)
  (photo 214804796). Public domain under
  [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): no credit line
  needed.
- `wolf-spider.jpg` (smoke test only, not in the store images): a female
  Carolina wolf spider (*Hogna carolinensis*) by codystricker via
  [iNaturalist](https://www.inaturalist.org/photos/227529967), also on
  [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hogna_carolinensis_female_dorsal.jpeg).
  Licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
