Get Waves alerts on your iPhone

Waves can send your visit reminders and other alerts through the Waves app. If they do not pop up on your iPhone, check these settings.

1. Turn on Waves alerts

  1. Open Settings, tap Notifications, then tap Waves.
  2. Turn on Allow Notifications. Under Alerts, check Lock Screen, Notification Center and Banners. Turn on Sounds.

If you see Immediate Delivery, choose it.

2. Let Waves through Sleep and Do Not Disturb

Reminders go out in the morning. If your Sleep or Do Not Disturb schedule is still on, your iPhone keeps the alert silent.

  1. Open Settings, tap Focus, then Sleep.
  2. Tap Apps under Allow Notifications.
  3. If it says Allow Notifications From, tap Add, choose Waves and tap Done.
  4. If it says Silence Notifications From, take Waves off that list.
  5. Do the same for Do Not Disturb if you use it.

3. Check the Waves app

Open the Waves app, tap Visits and scroll down to Service notifications. It should say "This device is connected." If you see Connect this device or Turn on app notifications, tap it.

Want to change which alerts you get, or get some by text instead? Use the choices under Service notifications in the same place.
