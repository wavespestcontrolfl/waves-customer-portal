# Trip link

The behavior below went live 2026-10-26 behind `GATE_TRIP_LINK` (removed from the code 2026-11-17; the gate no longer exists).

Owner "TRIP LINK" and "BOTH" 2026-10-26: real drive minutes, drive miles and GPS minutes on site per visit for the per-technician job cost, and a business or personal SUGGESTION per Bouncie trip for the mileage records. Shadow data only. A nightly job (01:40 ET: yesterday, then the day before again because a Bouncie trip event can arrive up to 12 hours late, then the worked day of any visit closed out yesterday up to 7 days back; job name `trip-link`, `server/services/trip-link.js`) and the script `server/scripts/trip-link-backfill.js --from YYYY-MM-DD --to YYYY-MM-DD [--dry-run]` (at most 120 days per run; the dry run prints the summary and writes nothing) take each technician with a Bouncie vehicle (`technicians.bouncie_imei`; a day that starts before `bouncie_imei_changed_at` is not judged and its visits read `no_vehicle`) and read that vehicle's trips and stops for the ET day from `bouncie_webhook_log` (the same trip-data read and 5 minute stop rule as the pin check, but starting 12 hours early so a trip across midnight is read whole; a trip belongs to the day it ENDS, the day of the stop it reaches, so a drive across midnight and its stop stay on one day; `server/services/bouncie-truck-stops.js`). Each COMPLETED visit worked that ET day (its arrival stamp, else its completion stamp: a visit closed out on a later day belongs to the day the truck was there; one physical visit per `visit_id` group; destination as the pin check resolves it, `server/services/visit-gps-context.js`) is matched to the stop inside the GPS arrival radius (`gps_arrival.radius_meters`, 175 m default) of its destination that overlaps the visit window, from 120 minutes before the arrival stamp (else the completion stamp) to 30 minutes after completion: the greatest overlap wins, a tie goes to the stop that began nearest the arrival stamp, and two still equal are `ambiguous`; none is `no_stop`, no pin is `no_destination`, no vehicle (or no technician on the visit) is `no_vehicle`. Visits that match the same stop split its minutes and carry `shared_stop_count`. `visit_gps_legs` (one row per completed scheduled service) stores the stop's arrival and departure, the GPS minutes on site, and the drive leg from the END of the vehicle's previous stop (the day's first trip start for the first stop) to the stop's start: minutes, miles (the sum of the `mileage_log` distances of the trips in between; NULL when any of them has no `mileage_log` row) and what the leg came from (`visit`, `supplier`, `business`, `personal`, `home`, `day_start`, `other`); grouped members of one visit share one leg. `trip_links` (one row per Bouncie trip) stores where the trip ENDED (`visit`, an active fence type, `home`, `other`) and `suggested_purpose` (visit, supplier and business fences = `business`; personal fence = `personal`; home and other = `unclassified`). A re-run replaces the day (upsert per scheduled service and per trip, one transaction per technician-day). It NEVER writes `mileage_log`, `scheduled_services`, `job_costs`, customer pins or alerts and sends nothing. `GET /api/admin/trip-link/summary?from=&to=` (staff only; at most 120 days: counts by status, matched %, medians of GPS and stamped on-site minutes, drive minutes and drive miles, trips and miles by suggested purpose and by end kind) reads the two tables. The two tables are derived data and the migration's `down` drops them.
