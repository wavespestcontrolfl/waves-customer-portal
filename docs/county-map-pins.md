# County map pins

The behavior below went live 2026-11-03 behind `GATE_GEOCODE_COUNTY_FALLBACK` (removed from the code 2026-11-17; the gate no longer exists). `GATE_GEOCODE_REVIEW`, `GATE_ROUTE_REORDER` and `GATE_ROUTE_REORDER_REPAIR` still apply as described.

Owner 2026-11-02 ("build pins"): the last 30 days put 7 of 69 new addresses on the staff `needs_pin` list (new-build streets in Manatee and misheard call addresses) and nobody works that list, while the free county roll places 6 of those 7. Needs `GATE_GEOCODE_REVIEW` (without it the write fences take no locks and the review rechecks are no-ops, so the county pass does nothing). Four things happen, none of them a Google call and none a customer message. (1) In `attemptReviewedGeocode` (needs `GATE_GEOCODE_REVIEW`), when Google returns a permanent `partial_match`, `no_result` or `coarse_result:*` for a complete address, the Manatee or Sarasota roll is asked for the lot (`lookupCountyLotPin` in `server/services/property-lookup/county-lot-pin.js`: house number plus one street word in one LIKE, situs ZIP must equal the address ZIP and the situs street name must hold that word as a whole word (the LIKE alone also matches a longer word), one lot or lots whose centers are within 60 m, center inside the service area). A lot saves the pin, mirrors it to the primary property, and records review status `geocoded`, reason and source `county_parcel`, evidence `{county, parcel_id, checked_at}`. No lot keeps `needs_pin` and records `county_checked_at` in the evidence. A county outage changes nothing. (2) The hourly geocoder backstop (`:20`, after the existing sweeps, `server/services/geocoder-county-fallback.js`) re-checks up to 10 `needs_pin` rows whose address is unchanged and that have no county check in the last 7 days. (3) The same pass pins up to 25 active `customer_properties` rows with no pin and that are not the primary row of the customer's own address: the customer's effective pin when the address equals the customer's (read through the review, so a pin the review withholds is never copied; a matching pinned primary counts, so such a row never waits on a customer sweep that will not run), else the county lot. The write runs under the background property write fence (`withReviewWriteFence` in `server/services/call-property-lookup.js`: try-locks on the customer and the row, so the pass never waits on a staff transaction; a held lock leaves the row for the next run), and lands only when the row, its primary flag, and the customer's address and pin are as read and no review holds a primary row's address. A county miss is remembered in memory and asked again after 7 days or an address edit; a pin the review withholds is asked again after 6 hours (a restart clears the memory). (4) Then up to 25 upcoming visits with no pin (next 30 days; pending, confirmed or a legacy NULL status; not locked or excluded from auto-dispatch) whose stamp names their active, pinned property take that pin through `persistServicePin` in `server/services/geocoder-service-locations.js`, the service-location backstop's write: technician-day lock, then the property share-locked (NOWAIT) and still at the address and pin read, the review recheck, the visit exactly as read, audit `service_coordinates_recovered` with source `property_pin`. Visits a review withholds are dropped before the batch limit (`filterServiceReviewBlocks`), so they cannot starve later ones. It needs `GATE_ROUTE_REORDER` and `GATE_ROUTE_REORDER_REPAIR`, like that backstop. Route quality then refreshes once for the dates touched. Charlotte, Hillsborough, a ZIP on a county line, and an address with no house number are never looked up.
