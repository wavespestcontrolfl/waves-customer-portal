# Weekly watering plans without email

This extends the saved-plan app workstream with the third package of the approved customer notification scope. An eligible recurring lawn customer can read the week's valid plan and receive its permitted property advisory while email is disabled or unavailable.

The existing Monday sweep publishes one authoritative customer-week decision behind `GATE_IRRIGATION_APP_PLAN` plus `GATE_IRRIGATION_WEEK_PLAN`. The sweep and plan readers use the same gate parser, including its supported `true`, `1` and `on` values. Email retains its separate `GATE_IRRIGATION_WEEKLY_EMAIL` and existing consent/suppression checks. Turning the email gate off does not disable plan publication; no additional cron, durable queue or notification preference is introduced.

When app publication is enabled, every eligible plan is published before the sweep awaits email delivery. Slow email providers and email attempt caps therefore cannot prevent later customers' app publication. Before delivering a prepared email, the same audience query rechecks its recipient and preferences; changed home or watering inputs withhold that delayed email. The existing queue-time claim and cutoff checks still run. With app publication disabled, the legacy email sweep continues sending at each customer's turn.

`irrigation_week_plans.published_at` records app availability. `sent_at` remains the real email outcome. Once published, the decision, weather, restriction and home inputs cannot be replaced or deleted by an email failure. A permitted email retry replays frozen weather against current settings and must reproduce the same decision before claiming the existing email lease. A changed home, schedule, policy or closed Monday window refuses the actionable email. A late/gate-off retry cannot substitute a contradictory legacy email for an already published plan.

Publication rechecks current eligibility, home/settings, policy and the Monday cutoff under the existing property-preferences advisory lock. Seasonal-tip opt-outs remain excluded. The property-alert engine still owns weather-alert opt-outs, quiet hours, weekly-plan priority and its shared seven-day cap. Weekly pushes require usable, confirmed portal watering numbers for the current home. The new account native-push preference is supplied by the separate notification-preferences PR; turning push off does not delete the plan.

The app and report readers accept a published plan or a legacy sent snapshot. Their existing policy, plan-week, current-setting and property checks remain in force. Report cache signatures and signed render pins use the stable availability timestamp; a later successful email does not change it. Readers reconcile a missing email stamp from the matching durable delivery even when the plan is already published; publication alone keeps the plan readable without creating an email outcome. Existing signed `plan` URL values keep working for older email snapshots. The app API retains an honest nullable `sentAt` and adds `availableAt`.

Customer merges preserve the actually emailed decision over a different app-only publication: a stamped email outranks a provider-accepted email awaiting its stamp, which outranks publication, then an unpublished draft. Ties retain the winner's row. A provider-accepted survivor receives its missing email stamp; publication alone never creates one. Every pre-plan email fallback checks availability again at the email queue boundary; an existing publication or an unreadable check withholds that fallback, including during gate rollback or a cutoff crossing.

Migration `20260907000050` is additive and uses the existing plan table. This PR extends the saved-plan screen already released in #4055. The existing app-plan gates also control this behavior, so release authorization is required before merging; this PR does not include a gate flip. Installed-device acceptance remains a separate release check. No production database or customer communication is used for validation.
